What's new in Dayframe.

v1.0.012 Nov 2026

Dayframe 1.0

  • First stable release. Same features as the preview, now versioned 1.0.0.
  • New privacy and terms pages on the website.
v0.1.012 Nov 2026

Preview release

  • Automatic tracking of the app in the foreground, sampled once a second.
  • Dashboard with a day timeline, per-app totals, active time and longest session.
  • Analytics across 7, 14 and 30 days, by category and hour of day.
  • Floating always-on-top widget showing your current app and session.
  • Idle detection: time stops when you step away; lock and sleep never count.
  • Consent-first onboarding, tray icon and optional start with Windows.
  • Settings to exclude apps, recategorise them and delete any date range.
  • Everything stays in a local SQLite database. No network, no telemetry.
