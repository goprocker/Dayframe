Privacy

How Dayframe handles your data. Last updated 12 Nov 2026.

01

What Dayframe records

  • The name of the app in the foreground, its window title and how long it stays there, sampled once a second.
  • Idle, lock and sleep periods, so they are left out of your totals.
  • Your settings: excluded apps, categories and preferences.
02

Where it lives

  • Everything is stored in a local SQLite database on your PC.
  • Dayframe makes no network requests and has no telemetry, analytics or crash reporting.
  • Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. We never see your data.
03

Your control

  • Tracking starts only after you consent during onboarding.
  • Exclude any app, recategorise it, or delete any date range from Settings.
  • Uninstalling Dayframe and removing its data folder deletes everything.
04

This website

  • This site sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address as part of the request.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.
